\begin{abstract}
Within the \BiDiSystem, the Coherence-Delta Calculus (CDC) is the formal source
language and executable native kernel for guarded hybrid state evolution. Its semantic center contains three primitive
reductions: synchronous phase flow, balanced-ternary commit, and nested
belief/prior exchange. Derived layers provide local observation, durable
effects, a guarded Universal Operator (U1), and a variational Universal
Operator (U2). U1 accepts a lifted double-cover closure only when reciprocal
relation roles, sheet restoration, holonomy, local commits, record/decision
agreement, and effect gating all pass. U2 differentiates that exact executed
path, then independently verifies complete or explicitly restored recurrence
before constructing a monodromy operator or emitting characteristic
multipliers. This distinction is operational: the canonical two-turn task loop
accepts U1 and yields a source-bound 13-coordinate tangent, but complete native
state recurrence is held because latch mode, belief, prior, and unwrapped phase
do not all return. Consequently, no canonical-loop multipliers are emitted. A
separate calibration fixture with an explicit $4\pi$ endpoint restoration and
complete derivative yields $M_{\mathrm{rel}}=I_{13}$ and a marginal spectrum.
The artifact binds results to canonical-source, state-manifest, tangent, return,
and spectrum receipts; exercises analytic, numerical, negative, sanitizer, and
permanent-mutant gates; and mechanizes only its precisely named finite
carrier, event-order, cone, sheet, aperture, and barrier statements in Lean and
Rocq. Richer continuous semantics and physical interpretations remain explicit
obligations rather than inferred conclusions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Hybrid software often needs continuous evolution, discrete admissibility,
nested reference frames, local observation, and effects in one auditable object.
Classical hybrid-automata theory supplies a rigorous setting for continuous
modes and discrete transitions \cite{henzinger1996}. CDC narrows that ambition to
a compact executable source contract whose higher-order claims are controlled by
receipts.

The contribution is a claim-preserving chain, not another syntax for a state
machine:
\[
\texttt{.cdc source}
\longrightarrow \text{typed state path}
\longrightarrow \Uop_1
\longrightarrow D\Uop_1
\longrightarrow \text{return gate}
\longrightarrow \text{spectrum}.
\]
Every arrow can hold without manufacturing the artifact to its right.

The release makes four contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a grammar-1 source and native runtime organized around exactly three
  primitive reductions;
\item U1, a guarded lifted-cover closure that is explicitly weaker than
  complete-state periodicity;
\item U2, a source-bound tangent and recurrence protocol that distinguishes path
  derivatives, projected returns, relative returns, monodromy, and Floquet
  multipliers; and
\item a two-axis evidence discipline separating evidence maturity from execution
  verdict, backed by deterministic receipts, counterexamples, and finite proof
  mirrors.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Evidence convention}

Each claim is reported as
\[
(\text{scope},\ \text{maturity},\ \text{verdict},\
 \text{receipt or obligation}).
\]
Maturity is one of \emph{specified}, \emph{executed},
\emph{adversarially verified}, or \emph{mechanized finite}. Verdict is one of
\emph{accepted}, \emph{held}, \emph{violated}, or \emph{not emitted}. These are
independent axes. A recurrence hold may be adversarially verified when the
implementation reliably refuses a false periodic-orbit claim. Conversely, a
mechanized finite involution says nothing by itself about a continuous physical
system.

The authority order is executed machine receipt, permanent verification gate,
versioned language/ABI contract, mathematical specification, and finally
presentation. A declaration, diagram, or screen cannot promote itself above its
receipt.

\section{Language and semantic partition}

The grammar contains declarations for fields, modules, cells, channels,
observers, effects, frameworks, U1, and U2. Its operational core remains:
\[
\operatorname{flow}(d),\qquad
\operatorname{commit}(m),\qquad
\operatorname{nest}(p,c).
\]
Trace/windows, councils, persistence, U1, and U2 are derived jobs.

CDC distinguishes a richer calculus specification from audited native
realization v1. The specification may name amplitude relaxation, delayed
history, belief flow, plasticity, projected relations, and localized events.
The current executor mutates a narrower state. For a selected U1 path, its U2
continuous vector is
\begin{equation}
x=
(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_{N_c},
 b_1,p_1,\ldots,b_{N_m},p_{N_m})\in\R^n,
\label{eq:state}
\end{equation}
ordered by source declaration. The discrete mode is
\[
q=(h_1,\sigma_1,\ldots,h_{N_c},\sigma_{N_c},
   \text{event itinerary}),
\]
where $h_i$ records latch presence and
$\sigma_i\in\{-1,0,+1\}$ is the latch value.

Amplitude, intrinsic frequency, precision, action gain, field constants, and
channel parameters are frozen for this tangent calculation. Source topology,
jobs, readout, and effect paths are fixed structure. Projected phase, winding,
sheet, and declared holonomy are derived values rather than hidden tangent
coordinates. The coordinate manifest closes over exactly the executed path;
unreferenced declaration islands cannot add inert unit multipliers.

\section{The audited native reductions}

\subsection{Synchronous phase flow}

Native v1 executes one synchronous finite-duration map. For a cell $i$ in the
selected field,
\begin{equation}
\theta_i' = \theta_i+\omega_i d
 +Gd\sum_{e:\,t(e)=i}w_e
 \sin(\theta_{s(e)}+\alpha_e-\theta_i),
\label{eq:flow}
\end{equation}
where only same-field channels contribute. Its exact phase Jacobian is
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial\theta_i'}{\partial\theta_\ell}
=\delta_{i\ell}+Gd\sum_{e:\,t(e)=i}w_e
\cos(\theta_{s(e)}+\alpha_e-\theta_i)
(\delta_{s(e)\ell}-\delta_{i\ell}).
\label{eq:flow-jacobian}
\end{equation}
All right sides read the same pre-step state. Beliefs and priors are unchanged.
Equation~\eqref{eq:flow} is the implemented map, not an asserted exact solution
of an idealized differential equation.

\subsection{Balanced-ternary commit}

With deadband $\delta$, the current quantizer is
\begin{equation}
\tau(\theta)=
\begin{cases}
+1,&\cos\theta>\delta,\\
-1,&\cos\theta<-\delta,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:trit}
\end{equation}
The ordered trit walk is accepted only when every prefix sum is nonnegative. An
accepted commit writes latches but does not change phase, belief, or prior. In a
fixed accepted trit/mode itinerary its continuous reset derivative is therefore
identity. A held commit terminates the accepted U1 path. At a quantization
boundary, or when a perturbation changes trits or event order, U2 holds rather
than differentiating across incompatible modes.

\subsection{Nested exchange}

Let $u(q)$ be the child mean trit selected from latches when present and current
quantization otherwise. Native v1 executes
\begin{equation}
b_p'=b_p+G u(q),\qquad p_c'=b_p'.
\label{eq:nest}
\end{equation}
Within one discrete mode, $u(q)$ is constant. Thus
\[
\frac{\partial b_p'}{\partial b_p}=1,\qquad
\frac{\partial p_c'}{\partial b_p}=1,\qquad
\frac{\partial p_c'}{\partial p_c}=0.
\]
The overwritten child-prior row is not identity. The release includes a
finite-difference oracle and a permanent mutant that substitutes an attractive
but unexecuted theoretical derivative.

\subsection{Specified but not yet executed semantics}

The source language can declare delay, amplitude, line projection, precision,
and richer guards. Native v1 does not dynamically update amplitude or channel
weight, does not carry delay history, does not apply line projection or openness
to Equation~\eqref{eq:flow}, and does not localize a guard crossing between
scheduled operations. These remain specified obligations. In particular,
nonzero delay requires an explicit history state, and the current source guard
cannot silently authorize saltation.

\section{Universal Operator U1}

U1 is a derived guarded composition of the three reductions. Let its state
component be
\[
\Uop_X:(q_0,x_0)\longmapsto(q_T,x_T).
\]
An accepted U1 requires reversed-endpoint receptive and radiant channels with a
shared pair identity, one-turn projection return with sheet inversion, two-turn
projection return with sheet restoration, holonomy agreement, accepted local
commits, equality of the computed record and decision coordinates, and
enactment of only that accepted coordinate.

This earns \emph{lifted-cover closure}. It does not imply
$(q_T,x_T)=(q_0,x_0)$. The distinction is material in the canonical example:
the cover projection and sheet close after two turns, while latches populate,
context belief and child prior accumulate, and the unwrapped cover phase advances
by $4\pi$.

\section{Variational Universal Operator U2}

\subsection{Path tangent}

For an accepted path with a stable mode itinerary, U2 may emit
\[
D\Uop_X(x_0):T_{x_0}X_{q_0}\longrightarrow T_{x_T}X_{q_T}.
\]
For column perturbations, operation Jacobians left-multiply in execution order.
For smooth segments $\Phi_k$ and event maps $S_k$,
\begin{equation}
D\Uop_X=\Phi_{N+1}S_N\Phi_N\cdots S_1\Phi_1.
\label{eq:path-tangent}
\end{equation}
The implementation updates $A\leftarrow J_kA$ from $A=I$. Noncommuting-event
fixtures and a reversed-product permanent mutant protect this convention.

\subsection{Reset and saltation boundary}

Scheduled commits use their reset Jacobian. A state-triggered hybrid event would
instead require the sensitivity jump \cite{hiskens2000,kong2024}
\begin{equation}
S_e=D_xR_e+
\frac{(f^+-D_xR_e f^- -\partial_tR_e)n^\top}
     {n^\top f^-+\partial_tg_e},
\qquad n^\top=D_xg_e.
\label{eq:saltation}
\end{equation}
CDC's generic U2 tests exercise this formula, including transversality and event
order. The canonical source path reports its guard method as
\texttt{unbound-no-saltation}, because its guard is not yet a localized
transition surface. This is a deliberate realization boundary, not missing
metadata.

\subsection{Recurrence before monodromy}

Absolute recurrence requires restored discrete mode, the same coordinate
manifest, a compatible itinerary class, and
\begin{equation}
\lVert x_T-x_0\rVert_W\leq\eps
\label{eq:absolute-return}
\end{equation}
under recorded absolute and relative tolerances. Lifted coordinates use
unwrapped distance; a sheet restoration cannot substitute for the continuous
residual.

Relative recurrence requires an explicit executable endpoint restoration
$\rho_\gamma:X_{q_T}\to X_{q_0}$, with
\begin{equation}
\lVert\rho_\gamma(x_T)-x_0\rVert_W\leq\eps.
\label{eq:relative-return}
\end{equation}
The receipt must bind the restoration action, complete derivative, and
equivariance/section witnesses. Omitting changed coordinates is only a projected
return and cannot authorize Floquet language.

Only a verified return constructs
\begin{equation}
M=D\Uop_X(x_0),\qquad
M_{\mathrm{rel}}=D\rho_\gamma(x_T)D\Uop_X(x_0).
\label{eq:monodromy}
\end{equation}
Characteristic multipliers are eigenvalues $\mu_i$ of the resulting square
operator \cite{dacunha2009}. After independently verified neutral modes are
removed, the release classifies a return as stable when all
$|\mu_i|<1-\tau$, unstable when some $|\mu_i|>1+\tau$, and marginal otherwise
when at least one mode lies in the unit band. A unit multiplier is not labeled
gauge by proximity alone; it requires a declared generator and section
alignment.

\section{Apertured oriented reciprocity}

CDC contains several compatible but distinct oriented structures:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier inversion exchanges $-1$ and $+1$ while fixing the aperture $0$;
\item receptive and radiant are reversed roles of directed relations;
\item path orientation may reverse angular direction; and
\item one turn flips double-cover sheet parity while two turns restore it.
\end{enumerate}
We call the U-level structure \emph{apertured oriented reciprocity}. It is more
precise than a global binary-polarity assertion: reciprocal channels need not
cancel, and their residual angular mismatch is retained as holonomy.

The distinctions are necessary. The two-symbol walk $+-$ passes the
nonnegative-prefix barrier, while its naive pointwise sign inverse $-+$ fails at
the first prefix. Carrier inversion is therefore not a global commit-safety
symmetry.

The implemented generic covariance gate accepts a supplied involution only when
it fixes the aperture and satisfies both
\begin{equation}
\rho_1\Uop_X(x)=\Uop_X^\rho(\rho_0x),\qquad
D\rho_1D\Uop_X=D\Uop_X^\rho D\rho_0.
\label{eq:polarity}
\end{equation}
Its analytic fixture and permanent mutants establish the mechanism. No current
\texttt{.cdc} form binds a conjugate operator to the canonical loop, and no
physical polarity law follows.

\section{Verification and formal layer}

The repository-wide gate rebuilds the grammar frontend, native runtime, unified
driver, store, and real-Schur path. It checks parser/registry parity,
canonical-source identity, replay and generated artifacts, U1 positive and
negative cases, U2 analytic and finite-difference oracles, deterministic
receipts, effect purity, sanitizers, persistence concurrency, and formal/paper
freshness.

The focused U2 gate includes eight permanent mutation families: reversed
product order, omitted saltation, omitted endpoint-restoration derivative,
invented nest derivative, false recurrence acceptance, ignored aperture,
skipped tangent covariance, and false polarity acceptance. Each mutant must be
killed by the same tests that accept the unmodified implementation.

The finite Lean and Rocq mirrors establish only their named statements:
balanced-ternary counts and finite algebra; tangent identity and associative
composition; observable order of two finite noncommuting maps; carrier, cone,
and sheet involutions; fixed aperture; and the $+-/-+$ barrier counterexample.
They do not prove the runtime Jacobian, recurrence, numerical spectrum,
source-bound covariance, continuous holonomy, or a physical theory.

\section{Artifact surface}

The release is intentionally source-centered:
\begin{itemize}
\item \path{kernel.cdc} and \path{laws.cdc} declare the language contract and
  16-key invariant registry;
\item \path{framework_loop.cdc} binds H5, U1, and the canonical U2 chain;
\item \path{runtime/cdc_native_runtime.c} executes primal, U1, persistence, and
  U2 source jobs;
\item \path{runtime/cdc_variational.c} and \path{runtime/cdc_linalg.c} implement
  tangent, recurrence, restoration, polarity, and validated real-Schur logic;
\item \path{tests/u2/} and \path{tests/fixtures/u2/} contain executable positive,
  negative, numerical, receipt, and purity cases; and
\item \path{formal/lean/} and \path{formal/coq/} contain the finite proof
  mirrors with explicit claim ceilings.
\end{itemize}

Within the language/runtime/semantic execution boundary, the one Python file,
\path{cdc_boot.py}, is a declaration/expectation oracle; separate
asset-generation utilities do not participate in execution;
it is not the reducer. The shipped native ABI is 1.5 and the source grammar is
version 1.

\section{Limitations and research boundary}

Version 0.3.0 does not establish global nonlinear stability, robustness outside
the tested neighborhood, arbitrary off-grid event execution, nonzero-delay
history dynamics, or parity between prose semantics and unimplemented primal
fields. The positive relative fixture validates a return-analysis mechanism; it
does not make the canonical loop recurrent.

The artifact is deterministic classical machinery. It provides neither a
physical constitutive law nor empirical evidence for spinorial matter,
nonlocality, gravity, plasma identity, biological identity, cosmology, quantum
hardware, or quantum advantage. Any such specialization requires a
domain-specific state map, units, constitutive grounding, calibration data,
competing models, and discriminating experiments. U2 makes those future claims
more engineerable and falsifiable; it does not pre-validate them.

\section{Conclusion}

CDC demonstrates that an ambitious hybrid calculus can preserve its full
research target while making the implemented claim boundary executable. U1
binds lifted closure to reciprocal relations, topology, record agreement, and
effect gating. U2 binds first-order analysis to the exact executed path and
refuses monodromy until state recurrence is independently earned. The
implemented result is neither a universal stability theorem nor a free-standing
numerical pipeline. It is a language-level discipline in which
accepted, held, mechanized, and exploratory statements remain distinct enough
to support both engineering and future formal or empirical specialization.
