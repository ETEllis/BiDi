# Cosmological Record Closure

Status: **constitutive hypothesis and executable research program; no
cosmological, gravitational, holographic, or Q-level claim is yet earned**

## 1. Thesis under test

Relational Record Closure proposes one scale-independent transition grammar:

```text
distributed possibility
  -> lawful interaction
  -> admissible distinction
  -> durable recoverable record
  -> local frame closure
  -> next-scale primitive
  -> return that changes future possibility
```

The strongest version is not ordinary simulation theory. It does not posit an
external computer, programmer, or more-real substrate running a copy of this
universe. It proposes that physical state and the relations through which state
can change are recursively co-produced:

```text
(state_t, admissible-relations_t)
  -> closure_t
  -> (record_t+1, admissible-relations_t+1)
```

Reality is therefore the substrate, evolving model, evaluator, and returned
constraint at once. This is called **constitutive self-simulation** in this
program. The term is earned only if the formal dynamics add predictions beyond
saying that nature evolves according to laws.

## 2. Frozen operator

For a bounded frame `F` interacting with an environment or neighboring frame
`E`:

\[
\mathcal U_F
=
\mathcal P_F
\circ
\mathcal R_F
\circ
I_{F:E}.
\]

- \(I_{F:E}\) is the lawful interaction.
- \(\mathcal R_F\) reduces the joint state into a locally maintainable record.
- \(\mathcal P_F\) returns the consequence of that record into the conditions
  governing later interactions.

Its only operational outcomes are:

```text
FLOW         continue lawful evolution without a closed fact
HOLD         preserve unresolved difference without mutation
COMMIT       persist an admissible shared record
RENORMALIZE  change frame, basis, or effective degrees of freedom
NEST         use a closed record as a primitive in another frame
```

No cosmological model may add a special outcome or silently change the meaning
of these outcomes to obtain a desired result.

## 3. Frame, boundary, and moving relational center

A frame is:

\[
F=(\Omega_F,\partial F,\mathcal A_F,\mathcal Z_F,\mathcal T_F),
\]

where:

- \(\Omega_F\) is the compatible state domain;
- \(\partial F\) is the declared causal/interaction boundary;
- \(\mathcal A_F\) is the algebra of locally admissible distinctions;
- \(\mathcal Z_F\) is the algebra of durable recoverable records;
- \(\mathcal T_F\) contains lawful translations to overlapping frames.

There is no privileged universal frame in this definition.

The moving relational center is the closed reference from which a subsequent
frame differentiates:

\[
0_{F+1}:=\Delta_F\Phi_\infty
\equiv [0_F\leftrightarrow\infty_F].
\]

Operationally:

```text
many compatible states in F
  -> one accountable record in F+1
  -> one primitive among many possibilities in F+2
```

Closure is locally one while functionally becoming the next scale's zero. The
center therefore moves whenever a new shared record becomes the reference
against which later differences are measured.

## 4. Outward return and dissipation

A closed record is not an endpoint. Its consequences propagate through
declared causal channels:

\[
(B'_F,Y_{F\to})
=
\mathcal P_F(z_{F+1}).
\]

The returned effect may alter:

- transition probabilities;
- accessible channels;
- effective coupling strengths;
- boundary capacity;
- topology;
- admissibility;
- or the basis in which later distinctions close.

The hypothesis that shared determinacy dissipates outward from a moving
relational center is represented by a measurable recovery profile:

\[
D_F(r,\tau)
=
I\!\left(
Z_F;
\operatorname{Recover}_{F\to G(r,\tau)}(Z_F)
\right),
\]

where \(r\) is relational separation and \(\tau\) is causal delay. A power-law
profile is a candidate result:

\[
D_F(r)\propto r^{-\alpha},
\]

not an axiom. Competing exponential, logarithmic, finite-range, topology-bound,
and scale-dependent profiles must be fitted without selecting the model after
seeing the target result.

## 5. Hidden granularity and the X dynamic

For reducer output \(z\), define the compatible hidden class:

\[
[x]_{z,F}
=
\{x\in\Omega_F:\mathcal R_F(x)=z\}.
\]

Frame-relative hidden granularity is:

\[
\Gamma_F=H(X_F\mid Z_F).
\]

Macro-actionability is:

\[
A_F=I(Z_F;Y_{\mathrm{future}})
\]

or its interventionist equivalent.

The X dynamic is the finite-regime duality:

\[
\text{less externally resolved micro-granularity}
\quad\leftrightarrow\quad
\text{more stable actionable macro-closure}.
\]

This is not universally monotonic. Too much compression destroys predictive
sufficiency; too much synchronization destroys differentiation. Each model
must search for and report the interior optimum rather than presuming it.

Three meanings remain separate:

1. unresolved granularity from a declared frame;
2. classical collective coherence of an order parameter;
3. quantum coherence of off-diagonal phase relations in a declared basis.

## 6. Quantum-classical reframe

The hypothesis is that quantum-like and classical-like behavior may be regimes
of relational record accessibility rather than two primitive substances.

Quantum-like regime:

- alternatives remain jointly actionable under lawful transformations;
- relative phase remains operationally accessible;
- no durable, redundant record has yet selected a stable local quotient;
- alternatives may recombine and interfere.

Classical-like regime:

- interaction has exported distinguishing information into durable records;
- a macro-equivalence class is stable and causally reusable;
- alternatives are no longer jointly accessible to that local frame;
- independently recoverable records make the closure effectively objective to
  participants sharing the record interface.

This does not earn a quantum interpretation merely by renaming decoherence.
The program must recover interference, record-conditioned visibility, Born
statistics, contextuality, composite-system structure, and Bell constraints.

## 7. Boundary accountability, not a two-dimensional screen

The holographic candidate is:

> For a bounded causal frame, all externally actionable distinctions may be
> representable by records recoverable through its boundary interaction
> channels.

This is **boundary record sufficiency**. It does not assert that reality is a
two-dimensional image or that information is literally stored on a material
surface.

Define closure capacity:

\[
C(\partial F;\epsilon,E,\mathcal T,\mathcal A)
=
\max |\mathcal D|,
\]

where \(\mathcal D\) is a set of independently recoverable and causally usable
record distinctions crossing \(\partial F\), subject to error \(\epsilon\),
energy \(E\), topology \(\mathcal T\), and authority/admissibility
\(\mathcal A\).

Area scaling, if present, must be derived:

\[
C(\partial F)\stackrel{?}{\propto}\operatorname{Area}(\partial F).
\]

The implementation must include controls whose channel count scales with
volume, edge count, cut rank, or a deliberately hard-coded area law.

## 8. Horizons

A horizon is:

> The boundary of a frame beyond which distinctions cannot participate in the
> frame's present shared-record algebra without a later lawful causal channel.

It is frame-relative and need not be a material surface. Horizon crossing
changes accessibility; it does not declare the crossed event unreal.

Each closure domain exposes:

- locally writable distinctions;
- locally readable records;
- a causal horizon;
- a hidden equivalence class;
- and translations to overlapping domains.

There is no privileged central ledger in the model.

## 9. Overlap consistency

If frames \(F_i\) and \(F_j\) can both recover a record concerning overlap
\(O_{ij}\), then:

\[
\operatorname{Recover}_{F_i}(O_{ij})
=
\mathcal T_{i\leftarrow j}
\left(
\operatorname{Recover}_{F_j}(O_{ij})
\right)
\]

within declared causal and error bounds.

A mismatch means one of:

- the record was not actually shared;
- a frame boundary was declared incorrectly;
- translation lost a required invariant;
- causal history is absent;
- or the proposed geometry is inconsistent.

## 10. Geometry candidate

Spacetime geometry may be the effective compatibility structure of overlapping
closure domains:

- adjacency: a lawful record-translation channel exists;
- causal order: one record can contribute to forming another;
- distance: minimum delay, cost, attenuation, or reconstruction difficulty;
- curvature: path-dependent translation or record holonomy;
- proper time: locally accumulated order of admissible closures;
- event: an equivalence class of mutually consistent record intersections.

For a loop of translations:

\[
\mathcal H_\gamma
=
\mathcal T_{1\leftarrow n}\cdots
\mathcal T_{3\leftarrow2}
\mathcal T_{2\leftarrow1}.
\]

Nontrivial \(\mathcal H_\gamma\) is a record-transport holonomy. It becomes a
geometry result only if it reproduces or predicts gauge-invariant observables,
not because the word curvature is attached to it.

interior hidden class, boundary record, and emitted record. No Page curve,

## 14. Constitutive predictions

1. **Boundary capacity:** recoverable information is controlled by causal-cut
   capacity rather than raw interior state count.
2. **Overlap consistency:** independently reduced frames agree on jointly
   recoverable records after lawful translation.
3. **Closure saturation:** excess incident relational complexity forces hold,
   renormalization, boundary expansion, horizon formation, or loss of
   recoverability.
4. **Turnover from transfer:** Page-like information turnover follows from
   recoverable record transfer, not a programmed curve.
5. **Record holonomy:** curvature-like behavior corresponds to path-dependent
   translation around closure-domain loops.
6. **Redundant closure:** classical accessibility tracks independently
   recoverable record redundancy rather than size alone.
7. **Macroscopic accessibility:** size alone does not eliminate quantum-like
   operation when distinguishing records remain inaccessible and reversible.
8. **Frame complementarity:** different interior/exterior descriptions can
   coexist when they agree on every lawful overlap.
9. **Relational dissipation:** joint recoverability decays with relational
   separation according to a measurable, non-presumed profile.

## 15. Refutation and anti-smuggling

The cosmological synthesis fails if:

- the operator changes meaning between models;
- boundary recovery uses hidden global state;
- a central ledger performs supposedly local reconstruction;
- area, Page, Born, Bell, contextual, or geometry targets are hard-coded;
- postselection or communication manufactures a witness;
- exponential state is hidden outside the resource ledger;
- “closure” predicts nothing beyond known decoherence/coarse-graining;
- overlap translations cannot be made mutually consistent;
- the return path does not measurably change later possibility;
- or accepted relativistic/quantum behavior cannot be recovered.

The operational C0-C3 runtime remains valid if a cosmological branch fails.
The stronger thesis does not get to protect itself by weakening its
falsifiers.
