# Relational Record Closure

Status: **classical runtime contract with separately gated physical
interpretations**

## 1. Constitutive operation

Reference-Frame Topological Coherence is governed by one recursive operation:

> Distributed possibility flows; interaction writes a durable distinction; the
> durable distinction closes a local frame; the closed frame becomes a unit at
> the next scale; and its consequences reshape the field that can close next.

For frame `F`:

```text
microstate and boundary
  --interact-->
candidate distinction
  --reduce and guard-->
durable record
  --project and enact-->
future field
```

The compact operator is:

```text
U_F = project_F o reduce_F o interact_F:E
```

with four permitted outcomes:

- `FLOW`: continue evolution without closing a fact;
- `HOLD`: retain unresolved difference without mutation;
- `COMMIT`: persist an admissible record;
- `RENORMALIZE`: change frame, scale, or basis because the current one no
  longer carries the interaction honestly.

A committed record may become incident plurality or a prior for a parent
frame. That recursive relation is `NEST`.

## 2. The X dynamic

Many distinct microstates may reduce to one macrostate. Two quantities must
remain separate:

```text
hidden granularity = information about microstate left unresolved by the frame
macro agency       = predictive and intervention value of the closed macrostate
```

Within a bounded regime, less resolved micro-granularity can coexist with more
stable macro-level prediction and control. This is not a universal monotonic
law. Excessive compression destroys predictive sufficiency; excessive
synchrony destroys differentiation.

The runtime therefore preserves both:

- a typed macrostate used for guarded action; and
- digests and provenance proving that its constituent microstates were not
  erased or silently identified.

## 3. Wave, event, and record

The runtime does not implement a literal wave/particle toggle.

The valid cross-domain grammar is:

```text
distributed evolution
-> typed physical or logical coupling
-> partial or complete loss of locally accessible alternatives
-> persistent record
-> conditional reuse by another frame
```

A measurement-like event is the moment a distributed possibility becomes a
durable, scale-relative record that can be conditionally reused by the next
frame.

This contract is compatible with several interpretations of quantum mechanics
but does not choose among them. The classical runtime cannot establish
wavefunction ontology, objective collapse, entanglement, Bell nonlocality, or
quantum advantage.

## 4. Per-event admissibility is not aggregate drive

The photoelectric effect separates photon energy from photon count: below the
work-function threshold, more photons do not substitute for sufficient energy
in one absorption event; above threshold, intensity changes event rate.

RFTC adopts only the architectural distinction:

```text
event arrival rate != per-event admissibility
```

The `packet-threshold` crucible witness therefore requires:

1. high aggregate input made from subthreshold packets produces no commit;
2. above-threshold packets commit at comparable probability under low and high
   flux;
3. committed count scales with flux;
4. per-commit excess remains unchanged.

A packet is not a photon, and a software commit is not a physical quantum.

## 5. A shared fact requires recoverable records

A producer-local label is not yet a shared fact. A shared record requires
independently addressable evidence that later participants can recover without
privileged access to the producer's private state.

The `record-redundancy` witness therefore compares:

- one fragment;
- a quorum of noisy, independently addressable fragments;
- scrambled fragments;
- a producer-only label with no fragment evidence.

The quorum must outperform one fragment, scrambled evidence must remain near
chance, and the producer-only label must be unrecoverable through the shared
record interface.

This is a classical distributed-record contract. It is not a test of quantum
Darwinism or an observer-independent ontology.

## 6. Mapping into the six RFTC forms

| Operation | RFTC form | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bound a writable/readable scale | `frame` | versioned membership, clock, freshness |
| Produce a maintainable macrostate | `reduce` | deterministic output plus microstate provenance |
| Declare incidence and boundary | `complex` | explicit orientation and adjacency |
| Protect or transition a sector | `topology` | invariant preservation and named phase slip |
| Admit a record or downward action | `authority` | scope, horizon, quorum, expiry, nonce |
| Share observations and receipts | `transport` | authentication, causal order, replay, partition hold |

No form may mutate state outside the existing guarded commit path.

## 7. Claim boundary

The software lane can earn:

- deterministic classical simulation;
- collective field computation;
- topologically classified logical cells;
- distributed, replayable BiDi logical-cell operation;
- benchmarked classical computational advantage.

It cannot self-promote to:

- nonclassical physical behavior;
- quantum superposition or entanglement;
- Bell nonlocality;
- quantum computational advantage.

Those require a genuine physical substrate and independently designed
loophole-aware witnesses.

## 8. Open physical debts

The following remain formalization targets, not identities embedded in the
runtime:

- whether gravity can be expressed as coupling among relational clocks;
- whether any macroscopic material system permits useful external dephasing
  while preserving internal closure;
- whether biological transducers show topology-specific responses beyond
  matched classical controls;
- whether a nonclassical substrate can satisfy the same authority, replay, and
  record contracts without changing their semantics.

The point of the runtime is to make these questions sharper. Operational value
must survive even if every stronger metaphysical interpretation fails.
