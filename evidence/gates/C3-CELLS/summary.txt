date: 2026-07-28
decision: D37-D38
abi: 1.5

## Claim

The local ABI can seal canonical oriented frame snapshots, classify and
witness topological transitions, preserve hidden provenance in logical-cell
state, execute a bounded deterministic recursive scheduler, supervise
authenticated commands with typed accept/hold/reject behavior, durably seal
complete signed envelopes, and reconstruct a fresh scheduler from exact
authenticated uncompacted history. D38 additionally binds atomic
configuration-epoch migration to one predecessor digest, canonical recursive
structure, and a canonically revalidated cell state. Journal restart replays
durable ACCEPT/REJECT outcomes under the configured peer policy.

This is a classical C3-capable local kernel. It is not a deployed multi-host
session, cross-host reconciliation evidence, public-key identity,
non-repudiation, a qubit, entanglement, nonclassicality, or quantum advantage.

## Permanent counterexamples

- unordered and cyclically rotated observations cannot alter the frame seal
- reversed orientation changes topology; a branch-cut edge is refused
- sector or membership change without the required witness is refused
- equal macrostate does not erase distinct hidden source provenance
- reordered graph construction and publication produce identical execution
- incomplete/old children cannot advance a composite
- mixed logical clocks cannot form one sealed frame
- a configuration-epoch batch with one invalid receipt imports no cell and the
  exact corrected batch may retry
- every exported epoch receipt binds its source scheduler configuration;
  individually valid cell receipts from two predecessors cannot be spliced
  into one epoch under a caller-selected digest
- a second import call with a foreign predecessor digest is also refused
- an unchanged composite cannot swap child bindings behind the same exterior
  oriented ring
- tampered structure, amplitude, lineage, transition, hidden-class, or state
  digest cannot import as pristine predecessor state
- bounded resource exhaustion is pre-mutation and retryable
- stale authenticated application work holds and remains exactly retryable
- malformed authenticated application payload is terminal without app mutation
- a subsequent valid causal command is not poisoned by that terminal rejection
- concurrent identical durable ingress seals once
- every store-commit crash boundary holds; exact retry succeeds after recovery
  whether the interrupted transaction was absent or durably latched
- restart re-authenticates every envelope and reapplies schema, recipient, key
  identifier, sequence, logical clock, and causal-parent policy; a wrong value
  or key mutates no scheduler
- terminal semantic rejection is a durable causal outcome but never an
  application event
- a valid early envelope followed by malformed authenticated history mutates no
  scheduler because the complete stream is decoded before publication
- duplicate durable delivery is idempotent
- committed corruption invokes no replay mutation
- compacted commitments are refused as non-executable history
- release, ASan/UBSan, and ThreadSanitizer lanes

## Composition boundary

`cdc_scheduler_journal_admit` is the supported durable ingress. The journal
owns its store transaction while admission is active; direct staging must not
interleave on the same handle. `cdc_scheduler_journal_replay` accepts only a
fresh sealed scheduler and exact uncompacted history. Actual network-session
identity and cross-host reconciliation remain the next C3 composition gate.
